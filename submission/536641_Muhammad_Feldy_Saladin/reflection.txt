Making a contract proof of existence in REMIX gave me a whole new perspective on how a contract system work on a block chain. There are many difficulties that I experiences while making the code, notably trying to get the code running in the first place, but I manage to do it, and I learned valuable lesson of the technical side of a block chain system.
